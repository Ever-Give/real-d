Cashier story:

I was a cashier at a sandwich shop. It was me and two other cashiers, some guy and some girl. The girl was cute. She was short, white, with black curly hair and eyeliner.

In this sandwich shop there is a bathroom that has a code that is used by both employees and customers. However, there are bathrooms outside the store as well but customers tend to go for the nearest ones, making it a little harder for employees to just run to the bathroom if it's busy.

So the male cashier goes to the bathroom. The female cashier told me that she was about to go but he went first. I didn't know what to say really so I asked if she was going to be ok. She said she was fine and it wasn't that bad.

The guy takes like a whole 15 minutes or more. He says he was on his phone when he came back. After dealing with some customers the girl told me she will be right back.

I thought it was over by then. Just slight need to pee from a cute girl and that's it.

But... I started to notice something...

She was bobbing up and down and fidgeting a bit. Which was strange. I thought she went to the bathroom already. I thought it was because of a draft or the cold. But later she kept bobbing up and down more. Then a customer asked her for the bathroom code. She told them the code with a slight strain in her voice. She bent down a little and starting bobbing up and down again. I asked her if she was fine and she said yes.

My theory is that when she went off to the bathroom it was occupied again and there might have been a line. So instead of waiting that long, she had to go back to the register. I couldn't really see what was going on in the bathroom from where I was standing.

This kept going for a few minutes. Customers would come in, we would do our thing, and she would fidget around a bit.

When there was no more customers, she left the register again. After a few minutes she came back fine. She was acting normal again.
