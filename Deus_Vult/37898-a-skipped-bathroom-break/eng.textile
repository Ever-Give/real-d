A skipped bathroom break

Author: Deus_Vult

*000*2017-06-24 20:48

I have a recurring dream where I desperately need to use the bathroom, and I keep looking and looking for one, but I can never seem to find one. All the bathrooms in the world seem to be too far away, or something is wrong, like it is locked. This story is a little like that dream, although it was my friend who desperately had to go, and I was just following along. The story goes something like this:

In my state, every high school student must take a certain number of state exams before they can graduate. I remember them being tedious things, where many students were packed into the gymnasium for 2 or 3 hours at a time on a Saturday or a Sunday.

I remember seeing a friend of mine, the girl I would eventually date in college, when I went to school on a Saturday morning to take one of these tests. We were not close friends at that time, but we knew each other well enough that when we saw each other we began to chat and to catch up. We talked for a while as we endured the purgatory that is waiting in the gymnasium with 200 other high schoolers at 8:00am on a Saturday morning, for a test that doesn't matter, and for a goal which seems unclear.

By 9:00am the test had begun. Typically we were given a bathroom break for about 10 minutes approximately an hour and a half in, but today we were not.

When everything was finished, by around 11:00am, I remember seeing my friend standing in a crowd with her legs crossed, and with her arms stretched down by her crotch with her hands clasped together as she made an exasperated face.

"Whats wrong?" I asked, already knowing the answer.

"I really need to go number one!" She whispered, which I thought was an odd way to put it.

I wasn't really sure what to say, so I said "I need to go too!", which wasn't even true. Regardless, we went to the bathroom together, and there was a massive line to the ladies room, which was out the door and had at least 10 or 15 girls in it.

"Lets try a different bathroom!" She suggested, seeing the line. I agreed, and we walked around the school looking for a different bathroom. We got to another one, and it was out of order. I joked around saying something like "That explains the bathroom line!". It wasn't a funny joke, but probably was true.

By the time we got back to the first bathroom the line had gotten longer, and there was now a long line for the mens room as well. She wasn't exactly happy, but she suggested that we leave the building and go somewhere else. I agreed, mostly because I had nothing better to do, and I wanted to see the story's conclusion. I didn't really need to go in the slightest.

We left the building and walked down the street. I tried to make small talk by saying things like "How do you think the test went" but she always responded with something like "Fine until they didn't give us our god damn bathroom break." or "I can't believe those fuckers didn't give us a bathroom break" or "Why the fuck were all the bathrooms out of order" etc etc. She was really mad.

We tried a convenience store, but they didn't have a public bathroom. Next we tried some kind of restaurant but it was closed. Finally we came to some odd grocery store, situated parallel to an ally, and which had only a single narrow isle which extended back a long way.

She asked where the bathroom was, and the clerk pointed to the back of the store, but suggested that someone was in there. She endeavored to wait.

I suggested in my previous story that I have seen my girlfriend so desperate that she did the pee dance on only two occasions. This was the first time, before we started dating, and the second time was in college when we played Edward Fortyhands - which is what my other story is about.

She grabbed her crotch with both hands and jumped up and down wildly from one foot onto the other while she was waiting. She knocked on the door in desperation a few times until finally the person came out and she rushed in.

I remember nonchalantly putting my ear to the door and hearing her go, and she really did have to go!

When she came out I had nearly forgotten that I told her that I needed to go, and when she held the door open for me to go in I was very confused for a few seconds until I remembered. I pretended to go, and flushed the toilet.

"Relieved?" I asked. She looked it.

She nodded her head and we went our separate ways.

The End.
