A Desperation Sighting at the Bookstore

Author: DesperateJill

*000*2022-01-09 12:27

I witnessed and experienced some desperation at the bookstore today, which now that I am out of my job is pretty much the only place I go all month long outside of the house, so it's pretty much my only chance to witness anything, so I suppose that I was fortunate to have a sighting that was pretty decent in all honesty.

Today when I went to the bookstore the ladies room was out of order, which is disappointing because I do like to pee seeing as I am usually at the bookstore for like three hours, as I definitely take my time there. But while I was there one woman went up to the door and she sort of hit her fist on the door and was like dammit I really had to go. Naturally that piqued my curiosity, like a lot, so I started to subtly follow the woman around. She was complaining to her friends that she had to go to the bathroom and they said that they would probably be leaving soon and she was saying that she hoped so because she really needed to go. I didn't want to seem like I was staring or stalking or anything, but I could see while she was talking with her friends it looked like they were arguing and it looked like she was crossing her legs and looking rather annoyed and everything.

Once again I didn't want to seem like I was following them or anything, so eventually it looked like she just left the store and I didn't really run into her again. Actually I did briefly a little while later where I could see that she still looked like she was agitated and encouraging her friends to leave and looking kind of bored and everything, so I'm guessing she probably wasn't a regular patron of the bookstore. But I could see that she was clearly crossing her legs and looking agitated. Far as I know she never got to go.

Towards the end of the night when the store was getting ready to close they finally did open the ladies restroom, so they must have fixed it but by then there was a long line, well not an insanely long line really, as the bookstore is rarely crowded, but there was a little bit of a wait, so it was nice seeing other women needing to go to the bathroom really bad by the end of the day. By then I had to go pretty badly myself seeing as I had not gone the whole time I was there.

I guess it's kind of a minor sighting, but seeing as I don't really get that many of them seeing as I rarely ever leave the house to go anywhere other than the bookstore the hospital now, it was nice to have seen something, so naturally I am in a bathroom frame of mind now tonight just when I wanted to try to get stuff accomplished!
